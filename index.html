Kids Story Bot

Kids Story Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload original animated story videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's channel. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people. Google sign-in credentials are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private repository. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms

For the owner's personal use only. Uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
